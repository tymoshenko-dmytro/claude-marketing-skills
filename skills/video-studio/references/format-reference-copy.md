# Format: "make it like this ad"

When the user sends a reference video, the default is to **copy it to the maximum**: the
dramatic arc beat for beat, the visual style (even if it is not your usual one), pace and
length, devices (a mascot hero, karaoke captions, a gag).

Three things are never copied:
1. Their aspect when your channel needs another (your aspect per channel is a brand law).
2. Their words - your script in their shape, facts and numbers from your product brief.
3. Their characters - your own (your identity: logo, mascot, colours).

Steps
1. Break the reference down first: a shot list with times, what each beat does, which moves
   from video-moves, the sound. A link: `/watch` with the Gemini engine sees picture and sound
   and answers with timestamps (setup in video-check); without it, video-check stills + a
   transcript. Write the breakdown to `reference/BREAKDOWN.md` in the film's folder.
2. Transfer table: their beat → your beat, with the claim check on every line you write.
   Translate their devices into your world's truth: a competitor's "physical failure" device
   (people not turning up) became management chaos on screen for a product whose customers'
   real pain is cost and overhead.
3. Build with the format the reference needs (code-built or generated).
4. Watch side by side before showing; the user compares against the reference.
